At Goldman Sachs, my work includes Jarvis, a compliance-wide platform for building, deploying, and scheduling DAG-based surveillances, and the migration of legacy surveillance systems to cloud infrastructure.

## The engineering work

- Built CI/CD pipelines for cloud and on-premises deployments, across production and pre-production environments.
- Integrated low-overhead logging into DAG workflows.
- Added region-specific holiday calendars for surveillance scheduling.
- Migrated Asset Management Compliance surveillance systems from ILOG, Sybase, and Hadoop MapReduce to Apache Spark, Snowflake, and AWS Glue.

## Reliability and outcomes

Pipeline enhancements and cloud migration reduced false positives by **90%** and improved surveillance job runtime by **14×**. Controls and automated retries helped achieve **95% SLA adherence**.

Weekly exception reporting was automated for compliance review, eliminating the manual review effort for that reporting workflow.
